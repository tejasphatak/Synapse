How it works

1

Open the site

No install, no account. Chromium-based browsers with WebGPU: desktop, Android, Xbox Edge.

2

Ask a question

The coordinator breaks the model into shards, routes your query through volunteer devices. Tokens stream back.

3

Contribute (optional)

Your GPU joins the pool to help others. Fully visible, one tap to stop. No hidden work.

Where it works

Today: Gemma 3 1B across a heterogeneous fleet — Qualcomm phones, Apple devices, NVIDIA laptops, Xbox via Edge, ARM tablets. Validated 99.99% numerically identical to the HuggingFace reference. Tomorrow: a classroom Raspberry Pi plus thirty tablets, no internet uplink. A rural clinic with three laptops holding a private fleet. A home where old phones still have a job.
